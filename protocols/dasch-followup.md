# DASCH follow-up of eclipse flags

Status: preparation for review. No observational result is claimed.

Freeze the top twenty ranking and its input hashes before plate inspection. For each target, record the Gaia/TIC/GCVS identity, angular offsets, original period and epoch, TESS sectors and candidate-screen evidence. An ambiguous identification cannot support a disappearance claim.

Fetch in small cached batches. The plate manifest needs plate ID, series, JD/time scale, quality flags, magnitude or limiting magnitude, comparison-star selection and cutout hash. Non-detections remain limits rather than missing rows. Fold accepted measurements at the historical period; check ephemeris uncertainty across the long time baseline.

Each target receives one of eclipses seen, not seen or too few useful plates, with the exact rule and supporting phase coverage. A photographic non-detection alone cannot show no eclipse. A second analyst repeats the cutouts and measurements from the manifest.

If no top-twenty target shows historical eclipses at the correct star, stop the method as specified in the project README. Target rankings, sharing agreement, plate products and human image inspection are prerequisites; this packet records no target verdict.
